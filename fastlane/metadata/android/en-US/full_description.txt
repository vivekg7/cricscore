CricScore is a quick, ad-free cricket scorer for street, gully and casual matches.

Set up a match in seconds: team names, overs and players per side. Player names are optional and can be edited mid-match.

Gully rules your way:
- runs for a wide or no-ball (0, 1 or 2) and whether it is bowled again
- free hit after a no-ball
- last man standing: the last batter can carry on alone
- retire a batter (they can come back later)

Scoring:
- big buttons for 0 to 6, out, wide, no-ball, bye and leg bye
- every dismissal type, including run outs at either end with runs completed
- undo any number of balls
- strike rotation, over changes and bowler prompts handled for you

Live screen with score, overs, current and required run rate, this over, and batter and bowler figures. Full scorecard with fall of wickets, partnerships, extras and bowling figures.

Share the scorecard as an image or as text. Match history is kept on your phone, and the match in progress survives the app being closed.

No ads, no tracking, no account, and no internet permission: nothing ever leaves your phone unless you share it.
