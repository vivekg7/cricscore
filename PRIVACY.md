# Privacy

CricScore does not collect, send or share any data.

- **No network access.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so
  Android does not let it connect to anything.
- **No ads, analytics, crash reporting or accounts.** There are no third-party SDKs
  in the app.
- **What is stored, and where.** Team names, player names, match settings and the
  ball-by-ball record of each match are kept in the app's private database on your
  device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. Android's own
  device backup may include this database if you have backups turned on; that is
  handled by your device, not by CricScore.
- **Sharing.** When you share a scorecard, the app writes a temporary image to its own
  cache and hands it to the app you pick. What happens after that is up to that app.
